# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-11-23

## English

Psynguechthyngue ("the app") is an independent Syncthing client for iPhone and iPad. It is designed so that your files and data stay under your control.

**We do not collect any data.** The developer runs no servers for the app and receives no information about you, your devices or your files. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

**How syncing works.** Files are transferred directly between devices you have paired, over encrypted TLS connections, using the open-source Syncthing protocol. To help your devices find and reach each other, the embedded Syncthing engine may use public infrastructure operated by the Syncthing project (not by us):

- *Global discovery servers* receive your device ID and the network addresses where your device can be reached.
- *Relay servers* may forward encrypted traffic between your devices when a direct connection is not possible. Relays cannot read your files.

Syncthing's anonymous usage reporting and crash reporting are permanently disabled in this app.

**Permissions.**

- *Camera*: used only to scan a QR code containing another device's ID. Images are not stored or transmitted.
- *Local network*: used to discover and connect to your own devices on the same network.

**Data on your device.** Configuration, sync state and diagnostic logs are stored only on your device (inside the app's container and App Group) and are removed when you delete the app. Logs are never sent anywhere automatically.

**Children.** The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.

**Contact.** For questions about this policy, open an issue at <https://github.com/simon300000/Psynguechthyngue/issues>.

## 简体中文

Psynguechthyngue（以下称"本 App"）是一款独立开发的 iPhone / iPad Syncthing 客户端，设计目标是让你的文件和数据始终由你自己掌控。

**我们不收集任何数据。** 开发者没有为本 App 运营任何服务器，也不会收到任何关于你、你的设备或你的文件的信息。本 App 不包含任何统计分析、广告、追踪或崩溃上报 SDK。

**同步如何进行。** 文件通过开源的 Syncthing 协议，在你已配对的设备之间经加密的 TLS 连接直接传输。为了让你的设备能够互相发现和连接，内置的 Syncthing 引擎可能会使用由 Syncthing 项目（而非我们）运营的公共基础设施：

- *全局发现服务器*会收到你的设备 ID 以及可以连接到你设备的网络地址。
- *中继服务器*在无法直接连接时，可能会在你的设备之间转发加密流量。中继无法读取你的文件内容。

本 App 已永久关闭 Syncthing 的匿名使用情况报告和崩溃报告。

**权限。**

- *相机*：仅用于扫描包含其他设备 ID 的二维码。图像不会被保存或传输。
- *本地网络*：用于在同一网络中发现并连接你自己的设备。

**你设备上的数据。** 配置、同步状态和诊断日志只保存在你的设备上（App 容器和 App Group 内），删除本 App 时会一并删除。日志不会被自动发送到任何地方。

**儿童。** 本 App 不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。

**变更。** 如本政策有变更，更新后的版本会发布在同一地址，并注明新的日期。

**联系。** 如对本政策有疑问，请在 <https://github.com/simon300000/Psynguechthyngue/issues> 提交 issue。
