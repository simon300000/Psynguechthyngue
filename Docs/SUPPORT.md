
# Psynguechthyngue Support / 技术支持

Psynguechthyngue is an independent Syncthing client for iPhone and iPad. It keeps folders in sync directly between your own devices, with no cloud account, and shows synced folders in the Files app.

Psynguechthyngue 是一款独立开发的 iPhone / iPad Syncthing 客户端：在你自己的设备之间直接同步文件夹，无需云端账号，并把同步文件夹接入「文件」App。

## Contact / 联系

Questions, bug reports and feedback: please [open an issue](https://github.com/simon300000/Psynguechthyngue/issues). If you are testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app (take a screenshot in the app, or open TestFlight › Psynguechthyngue › Send Beta Feedback).

问题、错误报告和建议：请[提交 issue](https://github.com/simon300000/Psynguechthyngue/issues)。通过 TestFlight 测试时，也可以在 TestFlight App 里直接发送反馈（在 App 内截屏，或打开 TestFlight › Psynguechthyngue › 发送 Beta 版反馈）。
